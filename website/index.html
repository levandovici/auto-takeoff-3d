First product · B2B

Takeoff Auto 3D

An automated takeoff and estimating tool for contractors, estimators and developers working with 3D building models.

3D model import

Open SketchUp models directly and inspect geometry, layers and structure — no manual redrawing.

Automatic quantities

Roof areas, surfaces and linear measurements are computed straight from the model — accurately, every time.

Material assemblies

Configurable material systems map every measured surface to the right products and labor items.

Deviz-ready estimates

Generate structured cost estimates (deviz) in Excel — internal SEM sheets and client-facing variants.

Client-ready PDFs

Export polished, branded quote documents your clients can sign off on — straight from the same data.

Scenario pricing

Compare material systems and price variants side by side to find the right margin for every bid.

Workflow

Three steps. Zero manual measuring.

  1. 01

    Model

    Open the project's SketchUp model. The app inspects the geometry and identifies roofs, surfaces and borders.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Quantities are computed automatically — areas, perimeters and volumes — and mapped to your material assemblies.

  3. 03

    Estimate

    Export a complete deviz in Excel and a client-ready PDF quote. Iterate pricing scenarios in seconds.
